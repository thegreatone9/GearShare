# Gear Share

An app to share all your gears and make money...

Dispute Resolution:
The process after an item is returned is governed by the Lender's decision regarding the security deposit.

Lender Review (Deposit Hold): After the Borrower returns the item, the dispute status is set to PENDING_DEPOSIT_RETURN. 
The Lender must inspect the item and either click "Settle" (releasing the deposit and setting status to COMPLETED) or click "File Claim" (retaining the deposit due to damage).

Dispute Activation (Borrower Action): If the Lender files a claim, the status moves to ACTIVE. 
The Borrower is alerted and must take immediate action by clicking "Submit Evidence" to upload counter-proof defending their deposit.

Admin Settlement: After receiving evidence from both parties, the Admin/Platform reviews the complete case file (claim, evidence, counter-evidence). 
The Admin then enforces the final decision (e.g., releasing funds, deducting damages, or requiring additional payment), setting the status to COMPLETED for final resolution.
